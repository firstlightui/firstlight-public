## Design Context

### Users

Firstlight UI serves Laravel and NativePHP developers building mobile applications. They come to the website to evaluate the package, understand its native implementation, explore its wider-ranging component catalogue, learn its familiar Blade API, and confidently adopt it in a real NativePHP project.

The website is an integrated marketing site, documentation system, and component showcase. Its primary job is to encourage developers to use Firstlight for device-native mobile UI and UX: genuine SwiftUI and Jetpack Compose controls exposed through conventions that feel at home alongside Laravel, Blade, Livewire, and NativePHP Mobile UI.

### Brand Personality

Firstlight is authentic, efficient, and reliable. It should feel like a credible, production-minded continuation of the Laravel and NativePHP ecosystem, not a separate frontend world or a superficial imitation of native UI.

The experience should build confidence quickly through clear language, visible implementation evidence, practical examples, and low-friction paths from evaluation to installation. Because it is a developer product, it may also reward exploration with thoughtful technical details, playful interactions, and moments of delight that never undermine clarity or trust.

### Aesthetic Direction

Firstlight should have a distinct identity that remains closely adjacent to the visual culture of Laravel and NativePHP. The name may inspire a restrained dawn motif: evolving light, horizon warmth, and the sense of a capable new beginning. The motif should feel intentional and technical rather than scenic, sentimental, or decorative.

Use a dawn-led palette instead of teal, pairing warm horizon accents with calm technical neutrals. Support equally considered light and dark themes from the outset. Do not reuse the untouched Laravel starter branding or treat the existing teal showcase theme as Firstlight's brand colour.

No logo exists yet. The system should leave room for a future wordmark and distinctive mark without making an improvised logo foundational to the first design pass.

### Design Principles

1. **Stay inside the ecosystem.** Use familiar Laravel, Blade, Livewire, and NativePHP language, patterns, and developer conventions so adoption feels continuous rather than disruptive.
2. **Prove that it is genuinely native.** Make the SwiftUI and Jetpack Compose implementations, platform-specific expression, accessibility, and behavioural evidence visible throughout the marketing, documentation, and showcase experience.
3. **Make the path to first success fast.** Prioritise concise explanations, copyable Blade examples, obvious installation steps, and progressive technical depth.
4. **Let dawn guide, not decorate.** Use light, warmth, contrast, and subtle transitions to create a recognisable Firstlight identity without relying on literal sunrise imagery or visual excess.
5. **Treat accessibility as correctness.** Meet WCAG 2.2 AA, support full keyboard use, preserve colour-blind-safe state communication, honour reduced-motion preferences, and maintain strong contrast in both themes.
6. **Delight developers with substance.** Add playful, crafted moments through code, native-device demonstrations, motion, and interaction, while keeping performance, reliability, and comprehension primary.
